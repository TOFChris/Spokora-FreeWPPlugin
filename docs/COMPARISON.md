# Why Spokora vs Yoast or Rank Math (without the news)

For operators who will still be here in 2027. The Rank Math supply-chain week is **timing**, not the product.

---

## One sentence

Yoast and Rank Math are excellent **SEO plugins**. Spokora is the **week**: SEO plus the other plugins you were going to install, with AI files machines actually fetch, and with **your** keys on **your** host when you want models or Search Console — paid once per seat, not per year per URL.

If the site only needed title tags, **stay on Yoast Free**. That honesty filters the buyer spokora.com already named.

---

## Where Spokora actually wins

1. **Stack weight.** The week still needs redirects, image/alt, llms, sometimes health and leads. Free aims to retire the extra **SEO + redirect** pair. Pro aims to retire the drawer.
2. **AI discovery as output.** Dual Search SEO + AI-readiness scores, `/llms.txt` hub, per-URL `/ai-docs/` and `/md/` — a deliverable, not only a checkbox that dumps a file. Incumbents will copy a dump; keep the pairing sharp.
3. **BYOK vs metered AI (Pro).** Their writing assistants bill inside their cloud. Ours uses the agency’s endpoint.
4. **No product account to run SEO (Free).** Rank Math Free is tangled with Rank Math accounts and “connect.” Yoast Free is closer to a classic WP plugin; Yoast Premium is still a subscription.
5. **Hijack + exact 301s in one house.** Switching cost is their moat. We already attack it on Pro; Free must keep it.
6. **Front-end can shut up.** Themes that already print schema (including TOF2) should not fight the SEO plugin.
7. **Lifetime seats (Pro).** Real math for 5–10 client sites vs per-site/year SEO suites.
8. **Self-hosted only, said plainly.** We will not win WordPress.com bakeries. We are not trying to.
9. **MCP / agent-ready (Pro).** Niche, 2026-shaped. Do not lead Free with it.

---

## Where they win (do not argue)

- Default install, tutorials, freelancer muscle memory.
- Rank Math’s schema **wizard** (FAQ / HowTo / Product boxes). Ours is a competent graph + overrides.
- Woo, local, news, video edge cases. A Shows CPT does not replace that — and Shows stays **out of Free**.
- Hosted GSC/analytics connect.
- WordPress.org until Free lists there. Until then we are “upload a zip.”
- Auto-updates. Pro still re-downloads from My Account as of 1.5.x. Their updater is why the supply chain hurt **and** why people use them.
- Finish vs breadth. Thirty-plus modules vs ten years of Woo variation checkboxes. Presets (“SEO only” / “client week”) matter more than another module.
